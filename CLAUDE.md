## Tank + city (kaiju mode)
- Tank `TW=120, TD=80, TH=56`. The old hand-placed 60×40 garden (log, rocks, dish, moss, ferns, plants) is mapped by `nat(x, z)` (scale `NS=1.3`) into the front-left; `inCity(x, z)` = back strip (z < -13) + right strip (x > 19) holds the ruined Japanese town (`js/city.js`).
- Buildings are walkable like rocks: game.js turns every `CITY.buildings[i].solid` into a height grid (`heightGrid()`, shared with ROCKS) in `SOLIDS`; `groundY` includes them. A building taller than `CLIMB (.35) × span` is a wall (steering + hard push in `drive()`/tick, `solidNear()`), lower ones the spider steps onto. Leg joints are pushed sideways out of walls (`solidPush()` in unclip/solve), a foot left behind a wall re-steps (`legBlocked()`), body rises early/at a capped rate on a roof edge.
- Spider span 5 at start, ×1.35 per molt up to `maxSpan × KAIJU (2.4)` (31–38, 6–7 molts). Speed grows sub-linearly; prey scale `p.k = span/12` (1–3.5; size, speed, food value). Bigger than `LOG_FIT (22)` it no longer hides in the log.
- Back wall (was cork): `city.js` builds a mossy retaining wall (擁壁) + ruined facades against the back glass, inside the merged BODY/DECO/MOSS meshes (no extra draw calls, not solid). Lid fixtures (`ledBar`, lamp hood, bulb) are hidden; their lights + god rays stay.
- Lots added in round 4 (all low, checked with a flood-fill for spans 5–38 so every street stays reachable): 2nd `store` (-6, -11), sheds (-30, -12.5) (-56, -12.8) (-53, 36.5), kiosks (-40, 27.5) (16, 37). A tall lot in front of the back street pinches it shut for spans 15–22 (the log blocks the detour) — keep new lots there low.
- Film cam (#tCine): street level, looks up at the spider, swings/pulls in until nothing blocks the view; fog ×2.4 and light shafts in the street gaps (`cityBeamMat`, in `beams`).

- `js/human.js` — ชัยภัทร (178 cm = 1.78 units: red graduation gown with gold bands/black+gold cuffs, white shirt, black trousers, curtain-fringe hair; medium-poly head, `HUMAN_GLASSES` flag; gown hem on its own swaying joint `J.hem`; walk 1.4 / sprint 7.5 then tires, hides behind buildings, speech bubble `humanSay`/`preyTalk`), `BLOOD` FX (splash / drip / stain fading ~45 s / silk `cocoon`). ## Navigation (round 4)
- Spider: `navGrid(L)` = 1-unit grid of cells a spider of span L can stand in (walls(L) inflated by .32 L, log box, tank edge) + connected areas (`comp`); `astar()` (8-neighbour, string-pulled); `cityRoute()` swaps any route leg that hits a wall for an A* path; `routeTo(to)` = planRoute (log) + cityRoute. `follow_route` watchdog: no progress 1.3 s → `unstick()` backs off the nearest wall (.7 s) and re-routes with forced A*; 2nd time drops the waypoint, >3 gives up. Arrival radius grows with span. Wander targets pass `goodSpot()` (free, same area, ≥ .36 L from tall walls).
- Prey: `preyBlocked()` / `openDir(p, sp, flee)` (16 headings: open ground up to 7 vs away-from-spider, avoids the heading it got stuck on). Watchdog in `Prey.update`: wants to move but moved < 30% in 1.2 s → turn out via openDir and hold it 1.5 s. Dubia/cricket flee use openDir.
- Test scripts (scratchpad, not in repo): flood-fill reach per span, headless stuck counters (spider 4 sizes × 300 s: 16 → 7; prey 12 × 180 s ×2: 156 → 35).

## Survival round (round 4, `js/survive.js` + game.js `CHASE`)
- Releasing ชัยภัทร starts `ROUND` (1 survival day = `DAY_S` 12 s × TM, `ROUND_DAYS` 30). Day 30 → `HELI.rescue` (he hides + keeps still, heli descends, `p.boarded`, win line). Eaten → lose line. Log only; bubbles for the rest (`humanSay` LOGGED set).
- `humanAI`: states hide / peek (ducks back if it sees/feels the spider) / trip (random nearest store = store/kiosk/食堂, or pond bank; detours when not urgent; mostly by day) / act / flee / wait. Human grid `humanNav()` (strips behind the back + east rows closed), `covered()` line-of-sight, `humanRoute()` avoids the spider. Needs `HUM.eatDays` 4 / `drinkDays` 3, at 0 only slower (×.8) + lower max stamina. Sprint 9.5 only when really hunted (stamina `sprintS` 6.5 s), jukes + a last-moment dodge on the lunge (`HUM.dodge`).
- Spider: hunts him when his steps are felt (radius `senseBase + L·senseL` × gait vib walk .6/jog 1/sprint 1.5, ×1.3 night) and hunger > `hungerD`/`hungerN` (day/night); prefers nearer insects. Chase: top = sprint × `overHuman`, slow `acc`, wide `turn`, A* round buildings, rushes to where he went quiet and feels around (`creep`, leg reach `touch`). Starving (hunger > 72, no other food): senses/runs/accelerates more (`starve*`), camps. Chase clock ends every chase in `tMin..tMax` (+2 starving) s → spider rests.
- Balance (headless, span 20, 60 rounds each, harness in scratchpad): keeper feeds → 63% survive; never feeds → 35%; chase median 11–13 s. Very sensitive to `hungerD/N` (45/29 → 57%, 48/32 → 88% fed).

## Scale + climbing
- World scale: a storey ≈ 3 units, a person ≈ 1.7; grass/leaves/twigs/sedges sized to that (big rocks kept as they were — changing them raised leg clipping before).
- Climbing (spider.js `poseRoot`/`foothold`): body height follows its target at a capped speed (≤ ~1.4 span/s up, 0.9 down → ≤ 0.05 span per frame), nose-up pitch at a roof edge ahead / nose-down going down (`fA − bA`, total pitch clamped), `climbLag` slows `drive()` while the body catches up; footholds far above/below the hip are pulled in (`rEff`, below-hip penalty) so knees keep their arch; hind feet stay on a roof until the hips pass the edge (SOLIDS only, not rocks).

## Science rules baked in
Vibration sensing (no good eyesight), external digestion, premolt fasting + dark abdomen, molt on back, soft pale fangs after molt (no feeding), low humidity → hard molt, live prey dangerous during molt, ectotherm speed ∝ temperature, nocturnal & light-avoiding, Old World = no urticating hairs, threat posture. Names: *Melopoeus minax*, *Chilobrachys huahini*, *Melopoeus lividus* (WSC 2026).

## Testing

Use `page.evaluate(() => document.getElementById('goBtn').click())` instead of `page.click`, long timeouts, collect `pageerror`. ## Known open items
Big spiders (≥ 31) can still dip a leg ~1–2 into thin low garden walls / rocks (~5% of span). No true vertical wall-climbing: tall buildings are walls, the spider only steps onto roofs lower than 0.35 × span (a wall-walking gait would be a new system). Log interior a bit dark; ferns slightly stylised (sway in wind + lean from spider body via shader, not per-leaf physics); big spiders' legs can clip log walls inside the tunnel; no sound yet.
